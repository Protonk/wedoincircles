# REFEREE-BOUNDARY-OBJECT-AUDIT

The strongest skeptical objection to the boundary object `(Z, ℱ, ν, δ)` doing real mathematical work, steelmanned in referee voice; the one-page reply that actually answers it; and the changes to the paper the audit motivates. Binding input to the refactor's staging pass.

## The objection, at full strength

> "The boundary object is a ledger in measure-theoretic clothing. The inverse limit is a formal product of six cost coordinates with no interaction; `δ` is defined as a max of pairwise rescaled differences; and the headline 'theorem' `δ ≥ (5π − 1) · Δ_{n_P}` is arithmetic of the authors' own normalization choices — the morphism rescalings are inputs to the construction, not discoveries made on it. Nothing constrains an actual algorithm to live on `Z`: all content hides in the `embed` map, which is specified at interface level. The measurability claims are near-vacuous — these are countable, essentially atomic spaces, on which every function is measurable, so 'reads measurably against `(Z, ℱ, ν, δ)`' costs nothing. The measure `ν` is decorative: nothing is integrated. The 'universality' of `Z` is relative to a diagram the authors chose, and they themselves prove the 'all reasonable morphisms' question is undecidable — so universality can never be cashed. In sum: the real mathematics is either in the substrate facts (proved, but elementary) or in the two open inputs (H1)/(H2); the object in between packages assumptions and returns them with interest that was deposited in advance."

This subsumes the weaker variants (why a measure at all; why σ-algebras on atomic spaces; isn't the floor true by definition). Any reply must answer the strong form.

## The reply (one page)

**First, concede what is true, precisely.** `Z` discovers nothing. It is a composition device, and the paper should say so in those words. The claim made for it is not that it generates theorems but that it is *the minimal structure under which the proved facts compose* — and that compositional work is real, because its absence is precisely the canon's failure mode. The four canon bounds are mutually non-transferring: there is no common coordinate in which Morgenstern's floor, Winograd's count, AFW's count, and the substrate facts can appear in one inequality. Without `Z`, Proposition F must be stated currency-by-currency, and each channel proposition then needs pairwise transfer lemmas to connect its channel to each currency's floor — transfer lemmas that the non-transfer results *forbid*. `Z` converts a forbidden family of pairwise transfers into a poset of declared rescalings, where comparison is licensed only along committed morphisms. This is the same kind of work a limit construction does in algebra: no new equations, but a universal object that turns a family of compatible maps into one site where a single argument can run. The test is counterfactual: delete `Z` and the ten-block proof of the Frontier Theorem shatters into twelve statements with no common subject.

**Second, locate the non-vacuous measure theory exactly — and agree the rest is cheap.** On atomic spaces, measurability-per-se is indeed free. The paper's measure theory bites in exactly two places, and the construction is audited against precisely those two. (1) *Pullback coarseness*: the Coarsening Theorem's content is that `R⁻¹(2^F)` is a genuinely coarse sub-σ-algebra of `2^L` and the three observables fail to factor through it — a factoring obstruction, which is the nontrivial direction; "measurable" is cheap, "factors through" is not. Faithfulness clause (i) is a factoring constraint, not a measurability claim. (2) *Non-nesting of hypothesis classes* for the three registers, with pairwise non-inclusion witnesses — again a structure of failures-to-factor. And `ν` is not decorative: the almost-every register's content is a full-measure statement, which has no home in a bare σ-algebra; `ν` exists because clause (ii) must encode an a.e. reading, and only there. The paper should state this division — two load-bearing facts, everything else bookkeeping — explicitly rather than letting the referee discover it.

**Third, own the normalization-relativity, and show where it does and does not matter.** The rescalings are inputs — committed from worked instances (`5π` from the best known chained route, against a provably optimal `4π` direct constant). What follows from commitments is exactly two things, of different strength: the floor's *value*, which is normalization-dependent and is fenced as such everywhere it appears (FLOOR-FORMULA-CONTRACT.md); and the floor's *positivity*, which is invariant under every positive rescaling and is what the impossibility consumes. The proof needs `δ ̸→ 0`; it never needs `5π` rather than `4.5π`. The referee's strongest sentence — "universality can never be cashed" — is the paper's own Proposition-named horizon: cross-chart invariance is stated as open and Rice-blocked, which is the difference between a hole and a fence. The theorem claims what the committed coordinate supports; the lift is named future work, with the reason it is hard proved rather than elided.

**Fourth, the package makes falsifiable contact, which an assumption-package cannot.** `δ` is not only a coordinate on `Z`; per its operational definition it is computable on actual schemes — the failure-to-agree of cocycle products across butterfly refinements, evaluated on schemes × inputs × precisions. The floor is therefore an empirical claim about real code: any concrete FFT-style pipeline (mixed-precision, autotuned, table-driven) embedded per the interface must exhibit `δ` at least the floor at threshold. A construction that merely repackaged its assumptions would return only its inputs; this one returns a number per scheme that the world can contradict.

**Summary sentence for the paper's own use:** the boundary object is to these lower bounds what a common refinement is to incompatible charts — it proves nothing by itself, and nothing here is provable without it.

## Changes this audit motivates (absorbed into PAPER-REFACTOR-PLAN S1/S2)

1. New §5 states the composition-device reading *in the paper's own voice* before a referee says it first: one block — "`Z` discovers nothing; it is the minimal structure under which the proved facts compose," followed by the counterfactual (what shatters without it).
2. New §5 (or §2) carries the two-fact division explicitly: the only non-vacuous measure theory is pullback coarseness and register non-nesting; faithfulness clauses are factoring constraints; `ν` exists for the almost-every register.
3. Near Proposition F: one block separating value (normalization-dependent, fenced) from positivity (rescaling-invariant, load-bearing) — this is also FLOOR-FORMULA-CONTRACT allowed-phrasing #2.
4. §7 gains the falsifiability point as part of "use": `δ` is computable on real schemes; the floor is checkable against actual FFT pipelines. (This is currently implicit and is the strongest single answer to "packaging.")
